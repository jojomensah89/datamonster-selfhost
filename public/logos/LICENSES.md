# Technology logo decisions

Reviewed design/dashboard-v2/assets/technology/sources.json on 2026-11-16. It records source URLs and hashes, but no redistribution licences. No prototype technology logos are copied into the application or public assets. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera, Safari, Android, Windows, Apple and Linux retain the existing BrowserIcon/OSIcon vector fallbacks until explicit redistribution permission and required notices can be established. This avoids treating a public download URL as a licence grant.
